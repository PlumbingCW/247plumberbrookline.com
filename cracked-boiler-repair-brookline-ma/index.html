Cracked Boiler in Brookline: Repair or Replace?

A cracked boiler is a safety issue first and a heating issue second. Here is what to watch for, what to do right away and how the repair decision works.

By the Brookline 24/7 Plumbing team · Published October 23, 2026 · Updated October 23, 2026

Call (617) 752-0573

A cracked boiler section or heat exchanger can leak water, let combustion gases into the room, or both. Treat it as a safety problem before it is a comfort problem. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or National Grid at 1-800-233-5325 before calling us. If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911.

Once the building is safe, the question is whether the crack can be repaired or whether the boiler has to be replaced. In most cases a cracked iron section or a cracked steel heat exchanger means replacement, though a few situations leave room for a repair. This guide explains how that decision works and what you can and cannot do yourself, which is nothing.

Safety first

What to do right now if you suspect a crack

Do these in order and do not try to finish the job yourself.

  • ✓Gas smell. Leave the building at once. Do not flip switches or use a phone inside. Call 911 or National Grid at 1-800-233-5325 from outside.
  • ✓Carbon monoxide alarm. Get everyone, pets included, into fresh air and call 911. Do not go back in to silence the alarm.
  • ✓Dizziness, headache or nausea at home. These can be early carbon monoxide symptoms. Leave and call 911.
  • ✓Water leaking from the boiler. Turn off the boiler's power switch and the fuel supply if you can reach them safely, then call dispatch.
  • ✓Do not run it. A cracked boiler should stay off until a licensed technician has looked at it. No tape, putty or sealant is a repair.

Signs

How a cracked boiler shows itself

Water pooling under the boiler or running down between sections is the most visible sign. Others are soot on the boiler or around the burner, a flame that looks yellow and lazy instead of crisp and blue, a pilot that will not stay lit and a pressure gauge that keeps dropping so the boiler needs constant refilling. We service systems for customers in Newton and Brighton as well.

A rumbling or kettling noise can also point to scale or a damaged exchanger. None of these alone proves a crack. They are reasons to shut it down and have a technician test it with a combustion analyzer and a pressure test.

Causes

Why boilers crack

Cast iron sections crack from thermal shock, when cold water hits a hot boiler or when the system is refilled after a low water condition. Freezing can also split a section when a building loses heat in winter. Steel and aluminum heat exchangers in newer boilers fail by corrosion, scale or overheating when circulation is poor.

Age and poor water treatment add to it. Boilers that are fed fresh water constantly through a leak build up mineral scale and corrode faster, so a small leak that goes untreated can turn into a cracked section.

The decision

Repair or replace a cracked boiler

Our boiler repair team tests the unit and tells you what the boiler can and cannot do. If the answer is replacement, plumbing installation covers the new unit and its piping, and your licensed plumber pulls the permit.

Steam or hot water

Steam versus hot water in older buildings

Steam systems heat water to boiling and push steam up to radiators, which then return condensate to the boiler. Hot water systems pump heated water through a loop. A crack in a steam boiler tends to show as constant makeup water and wet returns, and in a hot water boiler as a falling pressure gauge.

Many buildings put up before 1940 were first heated by steam or gravity hot water, and Brookline's median year built is 1938. If your building has cast iron radiators with one pipe and a valve on each, it is probably steam. Swapping a steam boiler for a hot water boiler means changing the whole radiator system, so the choice is not like for like.

No shortcuts

Why this is not a DIY repair

Sealing products and welding repairs on a cracked boiler can hide a failure until it releases combustion gas or scalding water. Gas, flue and permit work all require licensed professionals in Massachusetts, and homeowners cannot pull plumbing or gas permits in Brookline.

If the boiler is out and you need heat today, ask dispatch about options. Do not use a gas oven or an unvented space heater to warm a living space, since both can produce carbon monoxide.

Brookline note

Heating rules and housing that affect a boiler failure

Under the state's heating season guidance (105 CMR 410.201), the season runs from September 15 through June 15. Habitable rooms and bathrooms must reach 68 degrees from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. and 64 degrees from 11:01 p.m. to 6:59 a.m. Boards of Health may vary the dates. A cracked boiler that cannot be fired during those months can leave a unit below those minimums, so call quickly.
