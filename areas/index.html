Brookline and its neighborhoods

Brookline is home territory: Coolidge Corner, Brookline Village, Washington Square, Cottage Farm, Fisher Hill, Aspinwall Hill, Corey Hill, South Brookline and the Brookline side of Chestnut Hill. Brookline ZIP codes are 02445 and 02446, and Chestnut Hill is 02467.

Start with the full list of plumbing services or call (617) 752-0573 for emergency help.

Inside Brookline, the neighborhoods differ more than the map suggests. Around Coolidge Corner, Brookline Village and Washington Square, apartment buildings and mixed-use blocks are common, so shared stacks and risers are a regular concern. In the residential areas around Fisher Hill, Aspinwall Hill, Corey Hill and South Brookline, individual homes mean basements, boilers and sump pumps are the usual questions.

Newton

Newton is a city of thirteen villages, from Newton Centre to Waban and Auburndale, and it sits just west of Brookline. Our Newton page covers its ZIP codes and the plumbing issues that come with its older housing.

Newton's villages are Auburndale, Chestnut Hill, Newton Centre, Newton Corner, Newton Highlands, Newton Lower Falls, Newton Upper Falls, Newtonville, Nonantum, Oak Hill, Thompsonville, Waban and West Newton. Each is a short drive along Route 9 or Commonwealth Avenue, and dispatch routes you to the closest crew.

Boston neighborhoods next door

Brighton and Allston lie just north of Brookline across Commonwealth Avenue, and each has its own page. To the east and south are Jamaica Plain and West Roxbury.

These are Boston neighborhoods, so water and sewer service there comes from the Boston Water and Sewer Commission rather than Brookline's Department of Public Works. The pages explain who to call about what.
