Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we do with it.

Call (617) 752-0573

What we collect

When you call (617) 752-0573 we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us about the problem and your address. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This website does not have forms, accounts or comments. Our hosting provider records standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type in server logs.

How we use it

We use call information to dispatch a crew, give you a price and follow up on the job. We do not sell your information.

We may share job details with the plumber assigned to your address and with service providers who help us run the phone line.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will update this page.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you or to have it removed, email contact@247plumberbrookline.com. We reply within a reasonable time.
