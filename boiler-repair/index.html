Urgent

No heat right now: what to do first

A boiler that goes dark on a freezing night is more than an inconvenience, since pipes can freeze if the building stays cold. These steps keep you safe while you wait.

Smell gas? Leave

Leave the building and call 911 or National Grid at 1-800-233-5325 before calling us. Do not flip switches on the way out.

Carbon monoxide alarm sounding

Get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911. Do not go back in to reset the alarm.

Check the simple things

Confirm the thermostat is set above room temperature, the boiler switch is on, and the breaker has not tripped.

Protect the pipes

Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet drip if the building is dropping toward freezing.

Symptoms

Signs your boiler needs service

  • ✓No heat, boiler silent. A lockout, a tripped switch or a failed control board.
  • ✓Radiators warm at the bottom, cold at the top. Trapped air in a hot-water system, or a failing steam trap on a steam one.
  • ✓Banging in steam pipes. Water pooling in a pipe because of a low spot or a stuck vent.
  • ✓Pressure gauge off the normal range. Too high suggests a failing expansion tank, and too low suggests a leak.
  • ✓Water at the relief valve. The valve opened to protect the system.
  • ✓Short-cycling. The burner starts and stops every few minutes.
Diagnosis

How we find the fault

We start at the boiler with the pressure gauge, the water level in a steam sight glass, the flame color and the flue. Then we move to the circulator pump, the zone valves, the low-water cutoff and the thermostat wiring, because any one of them can silence a whole building.

A combustion analyzer reads the exhaust when a burner is running ragged, and a multimeter checks controls and transformers. We tell you which parts failed and whether a repair or a replacement makes sense.

Relief valve and expansion tank

Why the relief valve is leaking

A boiler relief valve opens when pressure climbs past its rating, and it is a safety device you should never plug. Two causes are common. The expansion tank may have lost its air charge, or the automatic fill valve may be feeding water in when it should not.

Replacing the valve alone fixes nothing if the pressure problem remains. We test the tank and fill valve and repair what is actually wrong.

Heating season

What the state's minimum heat rule says

The Massachusetts heating season runs from September 15 through June 15 under 105 CMR 410.201. During that period, habitable rooms and bathrooms must be kept at least 68 degrees Fahrenheit from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., and at least 64 degrees from 11:01 p.m. to 6:59 a.m., with a ceiling of 78 degrees. Boards of Health may vary the season dates.

If you rent and your boiler fails, tell the building owner or manager right away and keep a written record of when the heat stopped. We can document what we find at the boiler.

What affects the cost of a boiler repair
