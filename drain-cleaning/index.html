Emergency Drain Cleaning in Brookline, MA

A kitchen sink that will not empty, a tub with a ring of gray water, a toilet that gurgles when the washer drains. Dispatch picks up around the clock and sends a technician with cable machines and a drain camera.

Call (617) 752-0573

A drain that backs up into a sink or tub is an emergency in a Brookline apartment, and our 24/7 dispatch treats it that way. While you wait, stop running water in the unit, keep the dishwasher and washer off, and move rugs and electronics away from the wet floor. Skip the chemical drain opener, because it sits in standing water and splashes on whoever opens the trap next.

Drain cleaning means clearing a blockage from a kitchen line, a tub or shower drain, a floor drain or the main line that leaves the building. We pick between a hand auger, a drum cable machine, a camera and hydro jetting based on the pipe material and what is actually plugging it.

Symptoms

Signs the clog is deeper than the trap

One slow sink is usually a local clog. Several fixtures misbehaving at once points further down the line.

  • ✓Gurgling elsewhere. The toilet bubbles when the kitchen sink drains, which means air is fighting through a partial blockage.
  • ✓Water in the lowest fixture. A basement shower or floor drain fills when the washer or an upstairs unit drains.
  • ✓Slow everywhere. Tub, bathroom sink and toilet all drain sluggishly on the same morning.
  • ✓Sewer smell. A sour, rotten odor near a drain often means waste is sitting in the pipe instead of moving.
  • ✓Repeat clogs. You cleared it last month and it is back, so something in the pipe is catching debris.

Kitchen sinks

Kitchen sink clogs: grease, food and a long horizontal run

Kitchen lines clog more often than any other drain we open. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe, coats the wall and catches coffee grounds, rice and eggshell until the opening is the size of a pencil.

A small auger on a cordless drill clears most sink clogs in one visit. If the cable pulls out greasy gray sludge, we follow with a second pass so the pipe wall is scraped and not just poked through. The Town of Brookline's own sewer guidance says the same thing about grease: never pour it down a sink drain, and wipes do not break down in the sewer.

A garbage disposal that hums but will not spin is a separate repair, covered under garbage disposal repair.

Older buildings

Why shared drains in older Brookline buildings clog differently

Census ACS five-year estimates (2020 to 2024) put the median year built for Brookline housing at 1938, and about 77 percent of units sit in buildings with two or more units. Stacks in those buildings carry waste from several kitchens and baths, so a clog can start in one unit and show up in another.

If your ground-floor tub backs up when the upstairs neighbor runs the dishwasher, the blockage is below both of you. Tell dispatch which floor you are on and whether neighbors have the same problem. Renters should also call the landlord or property manager, since the shared line is usually their responsibility.

Snake or jet

Choosing between a cable, a camera and a jetter

The right tool depends on the blockage and the pipe. A cable breaks through. A jetter scours the wall. A camera tells us which one you need before we guess.

Bath and laundry

Tub, shower and washer drains

Tub and shower drains clog with hair wound around the stopper and soap film. Pulling the stopper and clearing it by hand is a fair first try, and we do the same before bringing out a cable.

A washer that backs up through its standpipe often has lint packed in a small 2 inch line. If the washer drains into the main stack, a clog there can push gray water up through a basement floor drain. A quick leak at a joint on a drain line shows up as a damp ceiling or a smell in a cabinet, and we check for it while the line is open.

Before we arrive

Drain backup: what to do first while we are on the way

Stopping the flow protects floors and neighbors. A few minutes of prep also speeds up the visit.

Stop adding water

No flushing, laundry, dishwasher or showers until the line is open. Every gallon you run has to go somewhere.

Protect the floor

Lay towels along the base of the wet fixture and lift anything electrical off the floor. Stay out of standing water near outlets.

Note what else is slow

Tell dispatch which fixtures are affected and whether neighbors or other floors are too. It tells us how far down the blockage sits.

Clear the path

Empty the cabinet under the sink and make room around the basement cleanout so the machine can reach the line.

Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and dispatch answers overnight and on weekends for backups.

Know before you call

What moves the price of a drain cleaning

You get a clear price before work starts. The number depends on a short list of things we can see or find out quickly.

  • Where the clog sitsA trap or the first few feet of a sink line is a smaller job than a clog 40 feet down a basement main.
  • AccessA removable cleanout is quick. A line with no cleanout may mean pulling a toilet or working through a roof vent.
  • Tool neededA hand auger, a drum machine, a camera run and a jetter each take different time and equipment.
  • Pipe conditionRough old iron holds debris and can need a second pass. A cracked section may mean a repair quote after the camera.
  • Time of dayOvernight and weekend dispatch is available, and the timing can affect the price.

Homeowner policies often exclude sewer or drain backup unless a separate endorsement was added. Check your policy before you file, and ask us for an itemized description of the work to send along.
