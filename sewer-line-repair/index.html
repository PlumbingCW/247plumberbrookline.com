Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Brookline, MA

Cleaning clears a clog. Repair fixes the pipe that keeps causing them. We locate the break on camera, explain the options in plain terms, and tell you which ones fit your lateral.

Call (617) 752-0573

A broken sewer line often announces itself with a backup that cabling will not fix, and our 24/7 dispatch takes that call at any hour. Until we arrive, stop using water in the building, keep people out of any wet basement, and do not run a snake or a rented jetter through a line you suspect has collapsed.

Sewer line repair covers the lateral that runs from the building foundation out to the public sewer main. That pipe is the owner's responsibility under Town rules. We camera the line, mark the failure, and walk through the repair choices: a spot repair, a lining, or replacing the full run.

Symptoms

Signs the pipe itself has failed

A clog is a blockage in a sound pipe. These signs suggest the pipe has changed shape.

  • ✓Cleared, then back. The line backs up again within weeks of cabling or jetting, usually at the same fixture.
  • ✓Cable stops short. A machine hits a hard stop at roughly the same footage each time, which often means a collapse or an offset joint.
  • ✓Wet or sunken ground. A soft patch of lawn or a dip in the driveway along the lateral's path.
  • ✓Sewer smell outdoors. Odor near the foundation or a lawn that is greener along one strip.
  • ✓Sewer line leak. A hole in the pipe leaks waste into soil, which may show up as damp basement walls near where the lateral exits.

Failure types

Collapsed, bellied, rooted or cracked: what each means

A collapse is a pipe that has caved in, so little or nothing passes. A belly is a low spot where soil settled under the pipe and waste pools, which collects paper and grease. A root intrusion is a joint that opened enough for roots to get in. A crack lets groundwater in and sewage out.

Clay and cast iron were common in older lines. Brookline's median home was built in 1938 per Census ACS estimates, so the original lateral under many houses is the one that matters. The camera tells us what you have.

Before we arrive

Collapsed sewer line: what to do first

If the camera finds a collapse we will tell you at the visit. Until then these steps limit damage.

Cut the water

Avoid flushing, laundry and showers. A broken lateral has nowhere to send the water.

Keep tools out

Do not feed a rental snake into a suspect line. A cutting head can tear a cracked pipe further.

Note the history

Write down when it backed up, where the cable stopped and any past root work. We use it to read the video.

Mark the yard

Show us where the pipe leaves the building and any soft or sunken ground along its path.

Repair choices

Spot repair, lining or full replacement

There is no single best option. The right one depends on how much pipe has failed, how deep it sits and what is above it.

Sewer repair options compared
OptionGood fitHonest trade-off
Spot repairOne cracked or offset section with sound pipe on both sidesDoes not fix the rest of an aging line
Pipe lining (cured-in-place)Long runs with cracks or root joints, or a pipe under a slab or drivewayNeeds a pipe that is still round and can pass a liner. Not for a full collapse

Rules

What Brookline requires before the dig starts

The Town's FAQ says a sewer pipe must be repaired by a licensed drain layer, and the homeowner owns the line from and including the connection to the public main. Plumbing permits are issued only to licensed plumbers, and homeowners cannot pull them in Brookline, so we handle that step.

Before any digging, Massachusetts law requires a call to 811, at least 72 hours ahead, not counting weekends and legal holidays. Because of that wait, an emergency often starts with a cleaning to restore flow, then a scheduled repair.

Under the slab

What about a line under the floor or a driveway?

A lateral under a basement slab or a paved drive is where lining earns its place, because it avoids breaking concrete. It only works if the camera shows the pipe is still round and not collapsed. We tell you if it does not fit.

Your water service line is a separate pipe from the sewer lateral, and it is covered under water service line repair. If the lateral leaves a West Roxbury house, Boston's rules apply and the owner is responsible for the entire lateral.

Know before you call

What drives the price of sewer line repair

We give a clear price before work starts. Repair quotes swing more than cleaning quotes, so here is what moves them.

  • Length of failed pipeA single cracked joint is a different job than forty feet of bellied pipe.
  • Depth and locationA shallow lateral in a lawn is easier than a deep one under a drive, a tree or a slab.
  • MethodTrench replacement, lining and spot repair use different crews and materials.
  • Permits and restorationPermits, inspection and putting soil, walk or pavement back are part of the total.
  • Street connectionWork near the public main may involve the Town, and that can affect timing.

Standard homeowner policies often exclude pipe wear and root damage. Some carriers sell a service line endorsement. Check before work, and get repair findings and video in writing.
