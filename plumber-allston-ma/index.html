Heating season

September 15 to June 15: when a cold radiator becomes a plumbing call

Massachusetts sets the heating season at September 15 through June 15 under 105 CMR 410.201. In those months the state minimum is 68 degrees F in habitable rooms and bathrooms from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., and 64 degrees F overnight. Local boards of health can vary the season dates.

Our part is the water side of the system. A boiler that loses pressure or weeps at its relief valve is a boiler repair job, and a tank that has gone cold is a water heater repair job. We fix the equipment and give a clear price before work starts. Who owes what between tenant and landlord is not ours to settle.

Deep freeze

Signs a pipe has frozen or split

In January 2014 a nor'easter followed by frigid temperatures broke pipes throughout Boston, according to CBS Boston. Any pipe in an exterior wall or an unheated basement is at risk, in a Commonwealth Avenue brick building as much as in a Brighton Avenue triple-decker.

  • ✓No flow. One tap or one floor has no water while the rest of the building runs normally.
  • ✓Frost or a bulge. Visible frost or swelling on a pipe in a basement or along an outside wall.
  • ✓A stain after the thaw. A new ceiling stain or drip once the building warms up, because a split pipe often leaks only after the ice melts.

Close the building's main water valve, open a tap on the lowest floor to drain the line, and call us. Never use a flame to thaw a pipe. Our burst pipe repair technicians cut out the split section and check the run on either side.

Thaw and storm season

Basements, backups and water from the street

Thaw is when split pipes announce themselves, and heavy rain or snowmelt is when a marginal drain finally backs up. The lowest fixture shows a blockage first, so a gurgling floor drain or a tub that rises while the washer runs deserves a call. Drain cleaning clears the branch line, and sewer line cleaning clears the run toward the street.

Not every wet basement starts inside. On December 8, 2017, a construction crew hit a water main at Brighton Avenue and Linden Street, and basement units flooded. For water coming from the street or sidewalk, BWSC's emergency line is 617-989-7000. For water from a pipe or fixture inside, close the valve and call us.

BWSC maintains the main in the street, and the owner is responsible for the entire lateral. A sewer camera inspection shows which stretch failed before anyone digs.

Move-in and move-out

Turnover weeks: what gets checked and what gets missed

Many Allston leases end around September 1, a stretch known locally as Allston Christmas. A unit left empty for a few days can show a dripping supply line, a running toilet or a water heater nobody turned back on.

  1. Run every tap. Hot and cold for a full minute, watching for sputtering or slow flow.
  2. Flush each toilet twice. Listen for a tank that keeps refilling after the flush.
  3. Look under the sinks. Use a flashlight and feel the supply lines and the trap for dampness.
  4. Inspect the water heater. Check the floor around the tank for a puddle or rust streaks.

Anything that fails belongs in a call, not a note for later. A running toilet wastes water every hour, and a damp supply line seldom improves on its own. Most toilet repair and faucet repair visits are short, and the price is clear before work starts.

Rental buildings

Tenants, landlords and property managers: how a call works

Allston and Brighton counted 21.0 percent of housing units as owner-occupied in the 2010 to 2011 planning profile, against 33.9 percent citywide. The data is old, but it explains why so many calls here involve an owner or manager who is not in the unit. Stacked units also share risers, so a third-floor leak often stains the ceiling two floors down, and leak detection with a moisture meter and thermal camera traces it before anyone cuts plaster.

Tell dispatch who is authorizing the work, and we give that person the clear price before we start. A tenant who finds a leak should close the valve and also call the manager or owner.

Utilities and permits

BWSC, MWRA and Boston ISD: who handles what

  • Mains and sewers. . Boston Water and Sewer Commission (BWSC) serves Allston. Its emergency number is 617-989-7000.
  • Water source. . BWSC water comes from MWRA's reservoirs. MWRA reports its water as soft, about 1 grain per gallon, so mineral scale is not the usual suspect in a failing heater here.
  • Lead lines. . BWSC has offered private lead service line replacement to eligible owners since January 1, 2023, with a lead hotline at 617-989-7888.
  • Permits. . Boston's Inspectional Services Department issues plumbing permits, and only licensed plumbers can pull them. Clearing a blocked drain or fixing a faucet needs none, while a water heater replacement does, and we handle that step.

What to do while you wait

Find the building shutoff

Switch off a leaking boiler

Clear the basement path
